About Me

I'm a full-stack developer who loves the process of building — from architecture decisions to the small UI details that make something feel finished. During my internship at the India Meteorological Department (IMD), I built a system that decoded raw SYNOP weather codes — data that, until then, only IMD staff could read — into plain, readable English, and shipped it as a working webpage.


Since then, I've built three full-stack projects spanning health-tech, meteorological data, and AI-powered travel planning. I'm big on hackathons and always looking for the next problem to dig into — currently on the lookout for full-stack/frontend opportunities where I can keep building things people actually use.
